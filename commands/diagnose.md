---
description: Diagnose why a campaign underperformed and propose a testable fix
argument-hint: <campaign name or description>
---

Use the `campaign-optimization` skill on $1.

Work backwards from revenue and conversion to find the actual failure point — audience, offer, timing,
channel or content. Never diagnose on open rate alone.

Produce one hypothesis with the evidence behind it and a change that can be tested, composing
`ab-testing` to size and bound the test if one is warranted.

Recommendation only until a human approves the change (`rules/safety-rules.md`).
