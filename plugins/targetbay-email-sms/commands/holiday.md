---
description: Decide whether a holiday is worth doing for this store, then build the sequence
argument-hint: <holiday or event, e.g. "Diwali" or "Black Friday">
---

Use the `holiday-marketing` skill to establish whether $1 is relevant to *this* store — its vertical,
catalogue, audience and prior results for that moment. "Not worth doing" is a valid and useful answer.

If it is worth doing, hand to `holiday-drip-campaign` to build the message sequence. Derive the stage
count from the holiday's sales window, product cycle, audience size and history. Never assume a fixed
number of stages.

Plan only. No campaign is created, scheduled or sent without explicit human approval
(`rules/safety-rules.md`).
