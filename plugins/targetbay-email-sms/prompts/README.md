# TargetBay Email & SMS prompt library

Copy-paste prompts for an AI assistant connected to the TargetBay Email & SMS MCP. Each one routes to a
single skill in this plugin, derives every threshold from your store data, and stops for your approval
before anything changes or sends. Replace any `[BRACKETED]` placeholder before pasting.

| Category | Covers | Prompts |
|---|---|---|
| [Campaign Creation](campaign-creation/README.md) | Shaping new campaigns: structure, subject lines, personalisation, launches and lessons from past sends | 7 |
| [Campaign QA](campaign-qa/README.md) | Checks to run on a built campaign, SMS or test before anything reaches a customer | 12 |
| [Lifecycle Flows](lifecycle-flows/README.md) | Automations that follow customers from signup to repeat purchase, lapse and recovery | 15 |
| [Peak Season](peak-season/README.md) | Planning, running and learning from your biggest sales period | 13 |
| [Reporting & Analysis](reporting-analysis/README.md) | Reading performance, finding what moved it and where the highest-leverage change is | 12 |
| [Segmentation & Audiences](segmentation-audiences/README.md) | Who to target: auditing, fixing and building segments from store data, and where they collide or tire | 12 |
| [Strategy & Planning](strategy-planning/README.md) | Setting the plan, the calendar and the priorities for what comes next | 10 |
