Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`content-optimization` skill), design the content structure for a reusable [CAMPAIGN TYPE, e.g. product feature, newsletter] email I can turn into a template.

Base it on my better-performing emails of that type and the audience they go to.

Give me, block by block in order:
- each block's job and what goes in it
- the lead, and the proof that supports it
- product blocks: count and order, derived from what converts in my past emails
- the single call to action and where it sits
- personalised blocks and each one's empty-data fallback
- accessibility notes

Then: what to fix outright, what to test, and claims needing verification. Direction, not finished design.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
