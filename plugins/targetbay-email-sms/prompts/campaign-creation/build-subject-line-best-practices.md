Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`content-optimization` skill), turn my past subject line tests into a one-page guideline for my team.

Use only my completed subject line and preheader tests, judged on clicks and revenue rather than opens alone. Note the sample size behind each finding.

Give me:
- patterns that won repeatedly, with evidence
- patterns that lost or made no difference
- findings too weak to rely on yet
- how results differed by audience
- a do and don't list in my brand voice
- questions still worth testing, so we stop re-testing settled ones

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
