---
title: Cross-Sell After the First Purchase
summary: Evidence-based next-category recommendations for recent buyers, with audience, moment and channel.
skill: cross-sell
---
Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`cross-sell` skill), find what my customers should buy next from a different category after a purchase.

Derive from my orders: which products and categories are actually bought together or in sequence, the typical gap before a second-category purchase, and which buyers stay single-category.

For each pairing give:
- the pairing and its evidence, with sample size
- audience, size and exclusions (returned it, already owns it)
- the moment and instrument: automation step or campaign, email or SMS
- an offer only if the evidence says it's needed
- expected effect, plus stock and margin risks

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
