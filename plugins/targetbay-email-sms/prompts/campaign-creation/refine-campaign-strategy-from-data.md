Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`campaign-optimization` skill), review my email and SMS campaigns year to date and explain what drove the strongest and weakest results.

Rank by revenue and conversion first, clicks second, never opens alone. Compare like with like: same channel, similar audience and offer.

For top and bottom performers, find where the difference came from (audience, offer, timing, channel or content), with sample sizes.

Give me:
- patterns that hold, with evidence
- hypotheses written as testable statements
- briefing rules for next quarter's campaigns
- results too small or noisy to act on

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
