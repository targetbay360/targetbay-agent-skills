Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`product-launch` skill), plan the launch of [PRODUCT OR COLLECTION] on [LAUNCH DATE].

Derive from my data: who is most likely to buy it (category affinity, response to past launches, value band), available stock, and what else is scheduled around the date.

For each wave give: audience, size, exclusions, channel, timing, and content direction (hook on the outcome, the problem it solves, proof, offer only if justified, one call to action).

Also give: the stock dependency, the follow-through plan once the launch spike fades, expected outcome per wave, and risks.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
