Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`list-hygiene` skill), flag deliverability risks across the segments I send to.

Derive per segment from my data: hard and soft bounces, complaint trend against my own variance, never-engaged contacts, engagement that rests only on opens (privacy-inflated opens prove little), acquisition source, and movement by mailbox provider.

Give me:
- current rates and the alert threshold derived from my history
- where the problem concentrates: segment, source or provider
- never-engaged, quiet-but-reachable and healthy groups, with sizes and prior value
- the suppression recommendation and its revenue cost
- a sunset path
- whether to keep sending meanwhile

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
