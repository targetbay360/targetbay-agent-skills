title: Check My A/B Test Before It Runs
summary: Whether a test's setup, sample size and decision rule can actually produce an answer.
skill: ab-testing
---
Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`ab-testing` skill), check my A/B test [TEST OR CAMPAIGN NAME] before it goes out.

Derive from my data: audience size per variant, the baseline for the outcome metric, and my normal week-to-week variance.

Tell me:
- the hypothesis restated, and whether only one variable differs
- whether the split, audience and duration can detect a meaningful difference at my volume
- the outcome metric (not opens alone), guard metric and decision threshold, fixed in advance
- whether a holdout is needed
- what a null result would mean
- if it can't resolve, what would make it resolvable

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
