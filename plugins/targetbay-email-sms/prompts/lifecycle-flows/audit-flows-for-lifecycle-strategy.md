Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`customer-lifecycle` skill), map where my customers actually sit in their lifecycle, using stage boundaries derived from my data rather than generic ones.

Derive from my order and engagement data: the gaps that separate new, repeat, at-risk and lapsed for my store, and how customers move between stages.

Give me:
- each stage boundary with the evidence for it
- customer count and value per stage
- transition rates and where they leak
- which of my existing flows serve each transition, and which are only transactional
- customers who fit no stage, if any

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
