Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`customer-retention` skill), find customers who are still active but going quiet relative to their own purchase pattern, and decide how to keep them.

Derive from my data: each customer's usual reorder interval, the signals that preceded past churn (longer gaps, falling engagement, smaller orders), and how many customers show them now.

Give me:
- the signal map: which behaviour triggers an intervention and when, derived from my history
- recommendations, each with the leak addressed, audience and size, instrument, expected effect with evidence, effort and risks
- my existing retention coverage
- what you rejected and why

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
