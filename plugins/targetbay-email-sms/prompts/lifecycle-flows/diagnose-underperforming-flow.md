Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`automation-optimization` skill), diagnose why my [flow name] flow is performing worse than it used to.

Compare its recent performance with its longer-run history, both windows derived from my data. Check for decay: expired offers, dead links, discontinued products, changed entry volume or a shift in who enters.

Give me:
- per-step loss profile for both windows
- what changed, with evidence
- the hypothesis and the smallest change that tests it, as a step or parameter delta
- effect on contacts currently in the flow
- measurement plan

If the flow's structure is the real problem, say so rather than tuning it.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
