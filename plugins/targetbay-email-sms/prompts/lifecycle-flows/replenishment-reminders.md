Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`product-replenishment` skill), work out which of my products are rebought on a real cycle and when to remind customers before they run out.

Derive reorder intervals per product and size from my order history. Exclude products without a measurable cycle and say why.

Give me:
- qualifying products with interval, size variant, sample size and confidence
- excluded products with the reason
- interval clusters and the journeys they imply
- the lead time before run-out and how you derived it
- channel per journey and audience sizes

If my catalogue is mostly durable, say so and stop.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
