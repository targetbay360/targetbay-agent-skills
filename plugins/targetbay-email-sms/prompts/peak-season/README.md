# Peak Season prompts

Planning, running and learning from your biggest sales period. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Build This Year's Peak Plan From History](build-bfcm-plan-from-historical-data.md) | A peak-season strategy grounded in last year's numbers, with what to change, what to keep, and the timeline. | [`holiday-marketing`](../../skills/holiday-marketing/SKILL.md) |
| [Choose Who Gets Early Access](identify-customers-bfcm-early-access.md) | Ranked, sized early-access audiences for your peak sale, with exclusions and channel eligibility. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Compare Seasonal Campaigns Year Over Year](compare-holiday-campaigns-yoy.md) | Year-over-year revenue and engagement for your seasonal launches, including whether timing explains the gap. | [`revenue-analysis`](../../skills/revenue-analysis/SKILL.md) |
| [Did My Peak-Season Buyers Stick Around?](determine-if-bfcm-buyers-stuck-around.md) | Whether new customers from your last peak came back, versus customers acquired the rest of the year. | [`customer-retention`](../../skills/customer-retention/SKILL.md) |
| [Find Revenue Leaks in My Peak Setup](audit-bfcm-strategy-revenue-leaks.md) | Where your current peak-season audiences, offers, timing and flows are losing revenue, ranked by impact. | [`holiday-marketing`](../../skills/holiday-marketing/SKILL.md) |
| [Get My Sending Ready for Peak Volume](warm-sending-reputation-peak-week.md) | A verdict on whether your sending can take a peak volume spike, and a ramp plan derived from it. | [`deliverability-qa`](../../skills/deliverability-qa/SKILL.md) |
| [Learn From Last Peak's Campaigns](extract-learnings-last-year-bfcm-emails.md) | What drove revenue and where fatigue showed in last peak's campaigns, turned into testable changes. | [`campaign-optimization`](../../skills/campaign-optimization/SKILL.md) |

| [Red-Team My Peak-Season Plan](red-team-bfcm-plan.md) | A pre-mortem on your drafted peak plan covering deliverability, cannibalisation, discount-training and leak risks. | [`holiday-marketing`](../../skills/holiday-marketing/SKILL.md) |

| [Turn Peak Buyers Into Repeat Customers](turn-bfcm-buyers-into-loyal-customers.md) | A post-peak retention plan that moves first-time peak buyers to a second, full-price purchase. | [`customer-retention`](../../skills/customer-retention/SKILL.md) |
