# Peak Season prompts

Planning, running and learning from your biggest sales period. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Plan This Year's Peak From Last Year's Results](plan-peak-from-last-year.md) | Last peak's evidence turned into this year's plan, with what to keep, what to change and the timeline. | [`holiday-marketing`](../../skills/holiday-marketing/SKILL.md) |
| [Choose Who Gets Early Access](pick-peak-early-access-audience.md) | Ranked, sized early-access audiences for your peak sale, with exclusions and channel eligibility. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Compare Seasonal Campaigns Year Over Year](compare-peak-campaigns-year-over-year.md) | Year-over-year revenue and engagement for your seasonal launches, including whether timing explains the gap. | [`revenue-analysis`](../../skills/revenue-analysis/SKILL.md) |
| [Did My Peak-Season Buyers Stick Around?](did-peak-buyers-return.md) | Whether new customers from your last peak came back, versus customers acquired the rest of the year. | [`customer-retention`](../../skills/customer-retention/SKILL.md) |
| [Find Revenue Leaks in My Current Peak Setup](audit-peak-setup.md) | Where the peak audiences, offers, timing and automations set up now will lose revenue, ranked by impact. | [`holiday-marketing`](../../skills/holiday-marketing/SKILL.md) |
| [Get My Sending Ready for Peak Volume](warm-sending-for-peak.md) | A verdict on whether your sending can take a peak volume spike, and a ramp plan derived from it. | [`deliverability-qa`](../../skills/deliverability-qa/SKILL.md) |
| [Learn From Last Peak's Campaigns](learn-from-last-peak-emails.md) | What drove revenue and where fatigue showed in last peak's campaigns, turned into testable changes. | [`campaign-optimization`](../../skills/campaign-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Pick Peak Offers That Protect Margin](choose-peak-offers-by-audience.md) | The right peak incentive per audience, including none, with depth, terms and the habit cost. | [`offer-strategy`](../../skills/offer-strategy/SKILL.md) |
| [Red-Team My Peak-Season Plan](red-team-peak-plan.md) | A pre-mortem on your drafted peak plan covering deliverability, cannibalisation, discount-training and leak risks. | [`holiday-marketing`](../../skills/holiday-marketing/SKILL.md) |
| [Sequence My Peak-Season Sends](build-peak-sequence.md) | A dated peak-season message sequence with stages, audiences, channels and a recovery window. | [`holiday-drip-campaign`](../../skills/holiday-drip-campaign/SKILL.md) |
| [Split Peak Sends Between Email and SMS](split-peak-sends-by-channel.md) | Which peak messages go on email, SMS or both, and how paired sends are ordered and spaced. | [`channel-optimization`](../../skills/channel-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Tune Abandonment Automations for Peak Week](adjust-abandonment-automations-for-peak.md) | Peak-specific timing and messaging changes for recovery automations, plus when to revert them. | [`automation-optimization`](../../skills/automation-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Turn Peak Buyers Into Repeat Customers](turn-peak-buyers-into-repeat-customers.md) | A post-peak retention plan that moves first-time peak buyers to a second, full-price purchase. | [`customer-retention`](../../skills/customer-retention/SKILL.md) |
