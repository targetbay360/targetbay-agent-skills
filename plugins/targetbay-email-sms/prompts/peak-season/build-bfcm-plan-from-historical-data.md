Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`holiday-marketing` skill), turn my last biggest peak season into this year's strategy.

Identify the peak from my order history, then run the numbers: which campaigns made the money, which angles worked, how frequency moved unsubscribes and complaints as the period built, and when fatigue set in.

Give me:
- things to do differently and things to keep, each with the numbers behind it
- relevant products and target audiences with sizes and exclusions
- objective and offer posture
- this year's timeline, derived from when my buyers actually bought
- at least one finding I wouldn't have guessed

Flag patterns resting on few sends as thin.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
