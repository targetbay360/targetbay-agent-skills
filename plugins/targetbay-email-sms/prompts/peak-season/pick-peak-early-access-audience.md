Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), decide who should get early access to my peak-season sale.

Derive candidates from my data: top customers by value, highly engaged contacts, and buyers who respond mainly to discounts. Size each and check for overlap.

Per audience:
- definition and the existing segment it maps to
- size and lookback window
- exclusions with reasons
- consented counts per channel
- rank and what drives it, with evidence
- risks, including leak and fatigue

Also audiences rejected, and why. If one audience is the right answer, say so.

Analysis only: change nothing.
