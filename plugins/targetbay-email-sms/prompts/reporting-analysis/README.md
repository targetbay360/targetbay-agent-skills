# Reporting & Analysis prompts

Reading performance, finding what moved it and where the highest-leverage change is. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [See Email and SMS Revenue Share Trends](analyze-email-sms-revenue-trends.md) | How much of your store revenue email and SMS drive, how that share is moving, and why. | [`revenue-analysis`](../../skills/revenue-analysis/SKILL.md) |
| [Find When Your List Grows Fastest](analyze-list-growth-trends.md) | Monthly email and SMS list growth, your seasonal spikes, and how new subscribers convert in each. | [`opportunity-discovery`](../../skills/opportunity-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Measure What a Promotion Really Earned](analyze-orders-by-tag.md) | A post-promotion breakdown of revenue, new versus returning buyers, top products, AOV and units per order. | [`revenue-analysis`](../../skills/revenue-analysis/SKILL.md) |
| [Diagnose Where Retention Breaks Down](analyze-retention-performance.md) | Retention by cohort and stage, the biggest leak, and what currently covers it. | [`customer-retention`](../../skills/customer-retention/SKILL.md) |
| [Learn Which Subject Lines Actually Convert](analyze-subject-line-performance.md) | Subject-line patterns from your own campaign history, judged on clicks and revenue, turned into testable hypotheses. | [`content-optimization`](../../skills/content-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Audit My Account for Opportunities](audit-account-for-revenue-opportunities.md) | A full-programme scan ranking your biggest revenue, retention and list-health opportunities by value and effort. | [`opportunity-discovery`](../../skills/opportunity-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Understand Why Your Best Emails Won](audit-top-performing-emails.md) | What your top-revenue campaigns share (audience, offer, timing, content) so you can repeat it deliberately. | [`campaign-optimization`](../../skills/campaign-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Separate Real Test Wins From Noise](build-ab-test-results-deck.md) | A verdict on each completed test, lift where it is real, and actions for your creative team. | [`ab-testing`](../../skills/ab-testing/SKILL.md) |
| [Find High-Volume Sends Wasting Revenue](find-revenue-leaks.md) | A ranked table of your highest-volume, lowest-yield campaigns with the failure point and fix to test. | [`campaign-optimization`](../../skills/campaign-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Track How Fast Subscribers Become Buyers](measure-signup-to-purchase-time.md) | The time from sign-up to first purchase, how it is trending, and where new subscribers stall. | [`customer-lifecycle`](../../skills/customer-lifecycle/SKILL.md) |
| [Pinpoint Where Post-Purchase Journeys Lose People](pull-post-purchase-flow-ctr-report.md) | A step-by-step report of your post-purchase automation, split by channel and customer type, with the biggest drop diagnosed. | [`automation-optimization`](../../skills/automation-optimization/SKILL.md) |
| [Get a Week-Over-Week Performance Recap](pull-weekly-performance-report.md) | A shareable weekly recap of campaign and automation results versus the prior week, leading with the biggest movers. | [`revenue-analysis`](../../skills/revenue-analysis/SKILL.md) |
