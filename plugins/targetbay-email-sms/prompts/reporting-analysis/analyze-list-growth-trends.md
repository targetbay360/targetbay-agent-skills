Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`opportunity-discovery` skill), analyse how my email and SMS lists have grown and what that means for where I focus.

Derive from my store data: net subscriber growth per month by channel, the campaigns and events around each spike, and welcome automation engagement and first-purchase conversion in high-growth versus normal months.

Give me:
- Growth by month and channel, with spikes marked
- Which periods predict high growth, with the evidence
- Whether new subscribers from spike periods convert better or worse
- List health signals that growth hides (unengaged share, complaints)
- Ranked opportunities, each with size, effort and owning skill

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
