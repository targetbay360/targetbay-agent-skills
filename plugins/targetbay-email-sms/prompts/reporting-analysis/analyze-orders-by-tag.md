summary: A post-promotion breakdown of revenue, new versus returning buyers, top products, AOV and units per order.

Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`revenue-analysis` skill), tell me what a specific promotion actually delivered. I'll name the promotion or discount code and its dates.

Derive from my store data: orders using that promotion, and a comparable prior period with no promotion as the baseline.

Give me:
- Revenue, orders, AOV and units per order, promotion vs baseline
- New versus returning customer split
- Top products bought under the promotion
- Revenue by campaign, automation and unattributed
- Whether the lift was incremental or pulled forward from normal full-price buying
- Confidence and limitations

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
