Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`customer-retention` skill), diagnose my retention and show me where I'm losing customers I still have.

Derive from my store data: repeat purchase rate by cohort and stage, my own repeat intervals, and which automations and campaigns currently target each stage.

Give me:
- Retention by cohort and stage, with the largest leak marked
- The coverage map: what runs against each leak, and how it performs
- Recommendations for the largest leak: audience and size, automation or campaign, expected effect from my data, effort, risks
- What you considered and rejected

Diagnosis first; don't build anything. If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
