title: Learn Which Subject Lines Actually Convert
summary: Subject-line patterns from your own campaign history, judged on clicks and revenue, turned into testable hypotheses.
skill: content-optimization

Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`content-optimization` skill), analyse the subject lines and preheaders from my past email campaigns.

Derive from my data which features are worth examining (length, specificity, urgency, offer mentions, personalisation, question form) rather than using a fixed list. Exclude sends too small or too unusual to compare fairly, and say which.

Give me:
- Patterns associated with better clicks, conversion and revenue per recipient, not opens alone
- Which patterns hold across audiences and which are one-offs
- Hypotheses to test, each with the metric that would judge it
- Variant directions for my next campaign across distinct angles

Flag where my volume is too small to conclude anything. If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
