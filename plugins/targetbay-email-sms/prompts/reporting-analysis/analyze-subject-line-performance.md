Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`content-optimization` skill), analyse the subject lines and preheaders from my past email campaigns and completed subject line tests, and turn them into a guide for my team.

Derive from my data which features are worth examining rather than using a fixed list. Exclude sends too small or too unusual to compare fairly, and say which.

Give me:
- patterns associated with better clicks, conversion and revenue per recipient, not opens alone, with sample sizes
- patterns that lost or made no difference
- which patterns hold across audiences and which are one-offs
- a do and don't list in my brand voice
- questions still worth testing, each with the metric that would judge it, so settled ones aren't re-tested

Flag where my volume is too small to conclude anything.

Change nothing until I approve.
