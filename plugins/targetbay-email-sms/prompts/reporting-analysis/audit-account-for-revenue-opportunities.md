title: Audit My Account for Opportunities
summary: A full-programme scan ranking your biggest revenue, retention and list-health opportunities by value and effort.
skill: opportunity-discovery

Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`opportunity-discovery` skill), audit my whole email and SMS programme and tell me what is most worth fixing or building next.

Scan everything before ranking: revenue, lifecycle leakage, automation coverage, campaign performance, list health, deliverability, channel mix, cadence, catalogue coverage, content, and campaign/automation overlap.

For each finding: what you see, why it matters, its size from my data, effort, risk, and the skill that should own it.

Group findings into quick wins, medium-term and strategic. Separately list what you checked and found healthy, and any area you couldn't scan and why.

End with: top opportunities ranked by value ÷ effort, and the one change to make first.

Analysis only: change nothing.
