Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`campaign-optimization` skill), explain why my best-performing email campaigns did well.

Derive from my store data: my top campaigns by revenue per recipient and conversion over a period you choose from my send history, compared with my typical campaign.

For each winner, walk the funnel and show which stage beat my baseline: reach, open, click, convert or order value. Then attribute it to audience, offer, timing, channel or content, with the comparison that proves it.

End with:
- The patterns shared across winners
- What was likely luck or seasonality rather than repeatable
- One hypothesis to test next, with its test design

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
