Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`campaign-optimization` skill), find where my high-volume campaigns are leaking revenue.

Derive from my store data: campaigns with the most recipients and the weakest revenue per recipient, over a period that gives each enough volume to judge.

In a table ranked by revenue a fix could recover, give for each:
- Recipients, delivered, click, conversion and revenue per recipient
- Where the funnel broke, compared with my baseline
- Likely cause: audience, offer, timing, channel or content
- The smallest change to test, with sample size and success metric

Mark any campaign whose sample is too small to diagnose. Triggered journeys belong in a separate automation review, so leave them out.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
