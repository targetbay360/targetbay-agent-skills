Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`revenue-analysis` skill), build my weekly performance recap for the week I name, compared with the week before.

Derive from my store data, separately for campaigns and automations, and split by email and SMS: recipients, delivered, unique opens (email), unique clicks, conversions, revenue, AOV and revenue per recipient.

Structure it as:
- Headline: the metrics that moved most week over week, and which term drove the revenue change
- Overall performance
- Campaign performance
- Automation performance by channel
- What explains the movement, and alternatives ruled out

Keep it short, in plain language a stakeholder can follow.

Analysis only: change nothing.
