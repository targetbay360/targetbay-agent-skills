# Segmentation & Audiences prompts

Who to target: auditing, fixing and building segments from store data, and where they collide or tire. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Keep, Fix or Retire Each Segment](analyze-segment-engagement.md) | A per-segment verdict on which recently used audiences earned their sends and which to stop. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Clean Up My Whole Segment Library](audit-all-segments.md) | Every segment flagged as duplicate, stale, too small, unused or worth keeping. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Turn Buying Behaviour Into Personas](build-psychographic-personas.md) | Customer personas drawn from my orders and engagement, each mapped to a segment I can target. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Build RFM Segments From My Orders](build-rfm-segments.md) | Recency, frequency and value groups from my order history, sized and ready to target differently. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Find Clickers Who Never Buy](convert-high-click-non-buyers.md) | A sized segment of frequent clickers who rarely purchase, with what they browse and why they hesitate. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Find Segments I'm Missing](discover-high-impact-segments.md) | Ranked segment opportunities my data supports but my account doesn't have yet. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Explain a Segment in Plain English](explain-segment-logic.md) | Who really qualifies for one segment, and whether its logic does what I intended. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Find Segments With Broken Logic](find-broken-segments.md) | Segments whose conditions contradict, return nothing, or catch the wrong people, with fixes. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Find the Customers Who Drive Revenue](identify-high-value-segments.md) | The audiences where my revenue and lifetime value concentrate, ranked, with how to treat each. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Rebuild Segments Around Purchase Behaviour](rebuild-segmentation-by-behavior.md) | A lifecycle segment architecture based on what customers buy, replacing open-based engagement tiers. | [`audience-discovery`](../../skills/audience-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [See Where Segments Collide Across Sends](check-segments-in-flows.md) | Which segments feed live automations and scheduled campaigns at once, and which send should yield. | [`campaign-conflict-resolver`](../../skills/campaign-conflict-resolver/SKILL.md) |
| [Spot Fatigue and Rebalance Email vs SMS](identify-subscriber-fatigue.md) | Segments showing contact fatigue, with a per-channel cadence budget that eases the load. | [`channel-optimization`](../../skills/channel-optimization/SKILL.md) |
