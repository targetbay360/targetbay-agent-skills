Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), judge the segments I've actually sent to recently.

Derive the lookback window from my send cadence and say what you chose.

For each segment sent to in that window give:
- size and channels used
- revenue per recipient, conversion, click and unsubscribe rate, with sample size
- where it drops off: delivered, clicked or purchased
- verdict: keep, fix (what to change) or retire, and why

Rank by revenue per recipient, not opens. Flag segments that overlap enough to double-contact the same people.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
