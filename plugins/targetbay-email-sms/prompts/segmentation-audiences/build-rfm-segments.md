Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), build RFM segments from my placed-order data.

Derive the recency, frequency and monetary bands from my own order distribution and repeat interval; state each cut point and why.

Group customers into champions, loyal, at-risk and lost, merging or splitting groups if my data says so. For each give:
- definition in business terms, and any existing segment it maps to
- size, lookback window and consented count per channel
- share of revenue
- exclusions
- what the group should be sent, and what it shouldn't

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
