title: Find Clickers Who Never Buy
summary: A sized segment of frequent clickers who rarely purchase, with what they browse and why they hesitate.
skill: audience-discovery
---
Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), find the customers who click often but rarely or never buy.

Derive "often" and "rarely" from my own click and purchase distributions, and state the cut points.

Give me:
- the segment definition: conditions, logic, time window
- size and consented count per channel
- the products, categories and content they click most
- the likely hesitation (price band, cart activity, discount history), labelled as inference
- exclusions, such as recent buyers and people already in a live automation
- the angle, offer need and channel to brief for converting them

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
