Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), find audiences my data supports that I'm not targeting yet.

Check my existing segments first so you don't suggest what I already have. Then look across lifecycle stage, purchase intervals, category affinity, value bands, discount dependence and channel engagement.

For each new segment give:
- name and why it matters
- definition and size
- exclusions
- the campaign or automation it would feed
- quick win or advanced, and what drives its rank

Rank by expected value. List the ideas you rejected (too small, overlapping, no evidence) and why.

Analysis only: change nothing.
