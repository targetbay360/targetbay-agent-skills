---
title: Find Segments With Broken Logic
summary: Segments whose conditions contradict, return nothing, or catch the wrong people, with fixes.
skill: audience-discovery
---
Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), check every segment for broken or flawed logic.

Look for:
- contradictory conditions or wrong and/or combinations
- segments that return nobody or implausibly many people
- references to products, lists or properties that no longer exist
- time windows that don't fit my purchase cycle, derived from my repeat intervals
- missing exclusions (suppressed, unconsented, recent buyers) where the intent needs them
- size swings that point to a definition problem

For each broken segment: the defect, who it wrongly includes or misses, the fix, size before and after, and which campaigns or automations use it.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
