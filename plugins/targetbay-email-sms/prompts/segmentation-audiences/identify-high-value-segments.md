Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`audience-discovery` skill), find where my revenue and customer lifetime value concentrate, so I know which audiences to protect and grow.

Derive from my data: value distribution, repeat rate, AOV bands, category affinity, discount dependence and engagement by channel.

For each high-value audience give:
- name, definition and size
- share of revenue and LTV, with the period
- the objective it serves: retain, grow or reactivate
- email and SMS approach, and what to avoid (over-discounting, over-sending)
- rank by revenue impact, retention impact and ease

Also flag valuable customers who are slipping away.

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
