For each high-value audience give:
- name, definition and size
- share of revenue and LTV, with the period
- whether it's slipping, steady or growing, with evidence
- email and SMS approach, and what to avoid (over-discounting, over-sending)
- rank by revenue at risk and ease of reaching it

Also tell me which valuable customers the push should leave alone.

Analysis only: change nothing.
