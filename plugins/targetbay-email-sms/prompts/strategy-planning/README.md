# Strategy & Planning prompts

Setting the plan, the calendar and the priorities for what comes next. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Check Bestsellers Before You Promote Them](analyze-bestsellers-and-refund-rates.md) | Your top products by revenue alongside refund rates and category shifts, so campaigns feature the right items. | [`revenue-analysis`](../../skills/revenue-analysis/SKILL.md) |
| [Find Offers That Earn Their Cost](analyze-offer-performance.md) | Which incentives your customers actually respond to, whether you need a discount at all, and what to use next. | [`offer-strategy`](../../skills/offer-strategy/SKILL.md) |
| [Map Your Marketing Year](annual-marketing-calendar.md) | A year-long calendar built on your own seasonality, with capacity for peaks, recovery windows and deliberate slack. | [`marketing-calendar`](../../skills/marketing-calendar/SKILL.md) |
| [Prioritise Tests Your Volume Can Resolve](build-experimentation-roadmap.md) | A ranked email and SMS test plan with hypotheses, sizing and a verdict on which tests are worth running. | [`ab-testing`](../../skills/ab-testing/SKILL.md) |
| [Build a Phased Retention Roadmap](build-retention-roadmap.md) | A sequenced plan of quick wins, mid-term programmes and strategic projects to keep customers buying. | [`customer-retention`](../../skills/customer-retention/SKILL.md) |
| [Get This Week's Highest-Leverage Revenue Moves](find-weekly-revenue-actions.md) | A short, ranked list of quick edits to campaigns, automations or segments that should lift revenue now. | [`revenue-growth`](../../skills/revenue-growth/SKILL.md) |
| [Set Up a New Store Right](first-week-setup.md) | What to build first on a new account, what must wait for history, and when to revisit. | [`store-onboarding`](../../skills/store-onboarding/SKILL.md) |
| [Stress-Test My Strategy Against My Data](gut-check-marketing-strategy.md) | Where your planned strategy misses the biggest opportunities or risks your store data reveals. | [`opportunity-discovery`](../../skills/opportunity-discovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Plan the Next 30 Days](plan-next-30-days.md) | A dated email and SMS calendar balancing revenue, list health and retention. | [`monthly-marketing-planner`](../../skills/monthly-marketing-planner/SKILL.md) |
| [Choose the Right Lever for AOV](raise-average-order-value.md) | Ranked ways to raise average order value, with thresholds derived from your order data and margin cost. | [`aov-growth`](../../skills/aov-growth/SKILL.md) |
