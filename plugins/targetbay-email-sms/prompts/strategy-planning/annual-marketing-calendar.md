title: Map Your Marketing Year

Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`marketing-calendar` skill), build my marketing calendar for the coming year.

Derive from my store data: my own seasonality from revenue history, committed launches and sales, and which holidays have actually earned revenue for me.

Give me:
- My seasonality map
- Each major moment with dates, objective, allocated capacity and status (committed, planned, candidate)
- Recovery windows after peaks, and unallocated slack
- Dependencies across periods
- Moments considered and declined, with reasons
- The assumptions each entry rests on

Keep it at the horizon level; I'll plan each month separately. If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Schedule nothing until I approve.
