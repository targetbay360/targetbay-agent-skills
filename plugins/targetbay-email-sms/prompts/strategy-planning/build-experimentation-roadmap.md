Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`ab-testing` skill), build a prioritised testing roadmap across my campaigns and automations.

Derive from my data: audience sizes, baseline conversion and revenue per recipient, and my past test history so nothing already settled is repeated blindly.

For each candidate test give:
- Hypothesis, dimension (offer, audience, timing, channel, content) and funnel stage
- Expected impact and effort
- Outcome metric and guard metric
- Sample size and duration needed, and whether my volume can resolve it

Rank by impact versus effort. List tests I should decide on reasoning instead because they can't resolve.

Change nothing until I approve.
