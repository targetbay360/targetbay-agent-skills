title: Build a Phased Retention Roadmap
summary: A sequenced plan of quick wins, mid-term programmes and strategic projects to keep customers buying.
skill: customer-retention
---
Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`customer-retention` skill), build me a phased roadmap for keeping existing customers buying.

Derive from my store data: retention by cohort and stage, my repeat intervals, the largest leaks, and what already covers each.

Organise recommendations into phases by effort (quick wins, mid-term programmes, strategic projects), merging any phase my data doesn't fill. For each give: the leak addressed, audience and size, automation or campaign, expected effect from my data, effort, dependencies and risks.

Rank within each phase by impact versus effort. Say which KPI tells me each item worked and what I should stop doing.

Change nothing until I approve.
