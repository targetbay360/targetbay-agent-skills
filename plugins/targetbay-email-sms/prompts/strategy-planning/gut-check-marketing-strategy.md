title: Stress-Test My Strategy Against My Data
summary: Where your planned strategy misses the biggest opportunities or risks your store data reveals.
skill: opportunity-discovery
---
Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`opportunity-discovery` skill), poke holes in the email and SMS strategy I'll paste below.

Before judging my plan, scan my store data across every lens: revenue, lifecycle leakage, automation coverage, campaigns, list health, deliverability, channel mix, cadence, catalogue and contention.

Then give me:
- Where my plan aligns with what the data says matters
- Opportunities the data ranks highly that my plan ignores, with size and effort
- Parts of my plan the data doesn't support, and why
- Risks my plan creates (fatigue, overlap, deliverability)
- The one change that would most improve the plan

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
