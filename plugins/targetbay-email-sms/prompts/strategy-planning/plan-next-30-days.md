Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`monthly-marketing-planner` skill), plan my next 30 days of email and SMS.

First derive from my store data: purchase frequency, key segments, seasonal moments, current cadence and lifecycle gaps.

For each send give: date, channel, objective, segment, offer (none unless justified), subject angle, why it earns the slot.

Balance promotional and non-promotional sends. Flag sends that should be automations and missing automations that would beat a campaign.

End with: the sends that matter most for revenue and for retention, as many as the data supports, and the biggest missed opportunity.

Change nothing until I approve.
