Using TargetBay Email & SMS (`aov-growth` skill), work out how I should raise my average order value.

Derive from my store data: the order value distribution and its clusters, items per order, average item value, and discount history by customer.

Give me:
- The distribution with dense clusters and gaps, not just the average
- AOV split into items per order and item value
- Levers ranked by net effect (thresholds, bundling, merchandising, tier moves, cross-category attachment), each with audience, size, revenue effect, margin cost and conversion risk
- Any threshold level, with its derivation shown
- Price-sensitive customers to exclude, and levers rejected

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate.
