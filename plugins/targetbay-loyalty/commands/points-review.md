description: Work out what a point is worth, what issuing it costs, and whether the arithmetic works
---

Use the `points-economics` skill.

Derive the point's monetary value from the reward catalogue rather than asserting it, and cost issuance
against margin rather than revenue. Every rate recommendation carries a liability projection over a stated
horizon (`rules/safety-rules.md#S3`).

Express reachability in orders, derived from this store's AOV — it is usually the binding constraint and
it is derived, never chosen.

Reject any proposal whose economics depend on points going unredeemed (`rules/economics-rules.md#E5`), and
state anything that reduces what members already earned as its own finding requiring its own approval.
