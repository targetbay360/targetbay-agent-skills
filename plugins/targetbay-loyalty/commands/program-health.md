---
description: Assess what the loyalty programme is actually doing, before anyone changes it
---

Use the `program-diagnosis` skill.

Report the participation funnel — enrolled, earning, redeeming — not the enrolment count. Read liability
with its ageing rather than as a total, because a large recent balance and a large old balance are
opposite findings.

When stating programme effect, name how selection was accounted for, or say plainly that it was not and
lower the confidence. Members outspend non-members in almost every store and almost none of that is caused
by the programme.

Name the binding constraint, and report what is healthy as well as what is not.

Analysis only — no configuration changes, no adjustments, no sends (`rules/safety-rules.md`).
