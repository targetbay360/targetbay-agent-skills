---
description: Find members who are leaving the programme and decide which are worth an intervention
---

Use the `member-recovery` skill.

Derive "quiet" from each member's own interval, not a store-wide threshold. Separate members who left the
programme from members who left the store — a points reminder does not recover the second group.

Classify by state (never activated, engaged-unredeemed, slowing, departed) and let the state choose the
intervention. State explicitly which groups are not worth contacting, and why.

Never manufacture urgency: an expiry or downgrade warning states a real date or a real threshold, or it is
not sent. Count programme messaging against the member's whole contact budget, not just the programme's.
