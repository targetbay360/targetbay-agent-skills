Using TargetBay Rewards (`program-diagnosis` skill), tell me whether my rewards program is actually incremental.

First derive from my store data: member and non-member order behaviour, behaviour before and after enrolment, and redemption patterns.

Give me:
- the effect estimate with its method
- the selection problem, stated plainly
- confidence, and what data would raise it
- liability and point value context

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Analysis only: change nothing and send nothing.
