# Member Engagement prompts

Winning back quiet members and running referrals that bring customers who stay. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Re-engage Quiet Rewards Members](reengage-quiet-rewards-members.md) | Quiet members grouped by what's really happening, with the right intervention for each group. | [`member-recovery`](../../skills/member-recovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Rescue Members With Expiring Points](rescue-members-with-expiring-points.md) | Members with points nearing expiry, prioritised, with an intervention that drives a redemption order. | [`member-recovery`](../../skills/member-recovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Keep Members From Losing Their Tier](keep-members-from-losing-their-tier.md) | Members close to a tier downgrade, and whether and how to intervene before it happens. | [`member-recovery`](../../skills/member-recovery/SKILL.md) |
| [Design My Referral Incentive](design-my-referral-incentive.md) | A referral incentive priced against what a referred customer is actually worth. | [`referral-program`](../../skills/referral-program/SKILL.md) |
| [Keep Referrals Paying for Real Customers](keep-referrals-paying-for-real-customers.md) | Fraud controls that stop self-referrals and junk signups, costed in legitimate referrals blocked. | [`referral-program`](../../skills/referral-program/SKILL.md) |
