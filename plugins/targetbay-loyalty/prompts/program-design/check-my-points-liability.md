Using TargetBay Rewards (`points-economics` skill), assess my outstanding points liability.

First derive from my store data: outstanding balance, its ageing, redemption rate and direction, point value and expiry policy.

Give me:
- liability in money with ageing
- which side is broken: issuance, reachability or catalogue
- options with their cost and liability projection
- any option relying on breakage, flagged and rejected
- member count and value affected by anything that reduces balances

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
