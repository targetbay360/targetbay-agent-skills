Using TargetBay Rewards (`tier-structure` skill), decide whether my program should have tiers and what shape they'd take.

First derive from my store data: customer value distribution, purchase frequency and any existing tier behaviour.

Give me:
- the clusters found, and whether tiers are warranted
- per tier: threshold, share of base, benefit, cost and behaviour it targets
- qualification window and downgrade policy with derivation
- any existing member whose tier would change

Change nothing until I approve.
