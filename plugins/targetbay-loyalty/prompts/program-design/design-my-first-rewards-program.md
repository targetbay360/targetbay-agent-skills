Using TargetBay Rewards (`program-design` skill), design the first version of my rewards program.

First derive from my store data: purchase frequency, AOV, margin, customer value spread and current consent coverage.

Give me:
- what it rewards and how members enrol
- entry reward and how many orders to reach it
- mechanics included, and those excluded from this version with reasons
- expiry and downgrade policy
- pre-launch baseline and measurement plan

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Change nothing and send nothing until I approve; show who and how many it affects first.
