# Tier Rules

Constraints on tier structure and benefits. Cited as `tier-rules.md#T2`.

---

### T1. Derive tier count from the value distribution, not from convention.
Read where customers actually cluster by spend or frequency. Tiers exist to name distinctions that already
exist in behaviour; inventing four tiers for a base with two natural groups creates two empty tiers and
one confused message (G7, G8).

### T2. A threshold members cannot reach is a threshold that demotivates.
Derive every threshold from the store's own spend distribution, and state what proportion of the base sits
below, within reach of, and above it. A top tier nobody enters is decoration.

### T3. Every tier needs a benefit members would notice.
A tier whose only benefit is a name does not change behaviour. If the benefit cannot be stated in terms a
member would care about, the tier should not exist (G8).

### T4. Benefits are costed per tier before the structure is proposed.
Tier benefits are ongoing margin commitments across everyone who qualifies. State the cost at the expected
population of each tier ([economics-rules.md#E2](economics-rules.md)).

### T5. Downgrade policy is decided at design time, not later.
How and whether a member loses a tier is part of the structure. Deciding it after members have qualified
means changing something they hold ([safety-rules.md#S2](safety-rules.md)).

### T6. Never restructure tiers in a way that silently demotes existing members.
A threshold change that moves qualified members down is a retroactive reduction. It is stated as its own
finding, with the affected count, and approved separately ([global-rules.md#G12](global-rules.md)).

### T7. The qualification window must match the purchase cycle.
An annual window on a store whose customers buy twice a year behaves differently from one whose customers
buy monthly. Derive the window from observed purchase frequency, not from a calendar year by default.

### T8. Prefer fewer tiers with real differences.
Where the value distribution is ambiguous, the smaller structure is the better bet: it is cheaper to add a
tier later than to remove one members have already earned.
