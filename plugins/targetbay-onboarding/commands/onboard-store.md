Onboard this store across TargetBay Email & SMS, Reviews and Loyalty, plus the onsite work that goes in
before any of them.

Work the onboarding pipeline in order. Start with `context-audit` to establish what is actually known
about the store and which facts are measured rather than borrowed. Run `onboarding-intake` for whatever
the platform cannot observe, asking only what the audit did not already answer. Then produce the
cross-product plan with `onboarding-blueprint`.

The onsite step spends no contact budget and cannot be added retroactively, so it sequences first. Route
it through `surface-inventory` — it reads consent, which bounds everything else the blueprint could
propose.

Stop at the approved blueprint. Provisioning is a separate request.
