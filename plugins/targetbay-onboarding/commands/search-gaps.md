---
description: Mine onsite search queries for catalogue gaps, vocabulary mismatches and ranking problems
---

Use the `onsite-search` skill.

Rank queries by volume times failure, not by failure alone — a query nobody types is not a problem.

Classify every failure before proposing a fix: a catalogue gap is a merchandising finding and is routed
out of search, not papered over with a redirect to something unrelated. Prefer synonyms over redirects for
vocabulary mismatches.

State what any ranking change demotes as well as what it promotes, and compare search conversion against
navigation conversion before concluding search is broken.
