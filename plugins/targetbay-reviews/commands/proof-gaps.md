Use the `review-coverage` skill.

Measure coverage per product, not as a store average, and weight every gap by the traffic or orders
actually behind it. Classify each gap as none, thin, stale, text-only or contested, because those have
different fixes and different owning skills.

State the pattern where one exists rather than listing product identifiers. Report what you could not
assess and why.

Analysis only — no requests, no sends, no changes (`rules/safety-rules.md`).
