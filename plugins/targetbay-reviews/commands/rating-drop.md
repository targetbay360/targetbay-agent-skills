---
description: Diagnose why a rating fell and decide what to do about the cause
---

Use the `rating-diagnosis` skill.

Establish the baseline first, then locate the movement in time and product. Read the actual review text
and extract the recurring attribute — the subject, not the sentiment — before naming a cause.

Route product, listing and fulfilment findings out of the review system. Select only the reviews where a
public reply changes something, and draft that text for a human to approve.

Never propose removing or suppressing a truthful review to protect a rating (`rules/safety-rules.md#S4`),
and never propose volume alone as the fix (`rules/response-rules.md#P8`).
