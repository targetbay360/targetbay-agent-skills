# Rating Health prompts

Understanding why ratings move and responding to the cause. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Find Why My Rating Is Falling](find-why-my-rating-is-falling.md) | The cause behind a falling rating, with evidence, and a response aimed at that cause. | [`rating-diagnosis`](../../skills/rating-diagnosis/SKILL.md) |
| [Group Complaints Into Fixable Causes](group-complaints-into-fixable-causes.md) | Negative reviews clustered by recurring attribute, each routed to the team that can fix it. | [`rating-diagnosis`](../../skills/rating-diagnosis/SKILL.md) |
| [Draft Replies to Reviews That Matter](draft-replies-to-reviews-that-matter.md) | A short list of reviews worth a public reply, with drafts for me to approve one by one. | [`rating-diagnosis`](../../skills/rating-diagnosis/SKILL.md) |
| [Turn Reviews Into Customer Insight](decode-customer-reviews.md) | What customers praise and complain about, in their own words, routed to product and listing fixes. | [`rating-diagnosis`](../../skills/rating-diagnosis/SKILL.md) |
