Using TargetBay Reviews (`rating-diagnosis` skill), decode what my reviews say about my products and customers.

First derive from my store data: review text and ratings by product and period, especially low and mixed ratings.

Give me:
- recurring attributes praised and criticised, with frequency and verbatim quotes
- where the listing promises something reviews contradict
- the classified cause behind each complaint theme
- findings for product, listing and fulfilment teams

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Analysis only: change nothing and send nothing.
