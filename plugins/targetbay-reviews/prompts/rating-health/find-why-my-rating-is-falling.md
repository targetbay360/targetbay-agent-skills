title: Find Why My Rating Is Falling
summary: The cause behind a falling rating, with evidence, and a response aimed at that cause.
skill: rating-diagnosis
---
Using TargetBay Reviews (`rating-diagnosis` skill), find out why my rating dropped and what to do about the cause, not the number.

First derive from my store data: my baseline rating distribution, when and on which products it moved, what low reviews say, and what changed around then.

Give me:
- the movement: period, products, distribution shift
- the classified cause (product, fulfilment, expectation or isolated) with evidence and confidence
- fixes that belong outside reviews: product, listing or fulfilment
- which reviews, if any, deserve a public reply

Change nothing until I approve.
