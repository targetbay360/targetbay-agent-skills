# Social Proof prompts

Finding where proof is missing and putting existing proof where shoppers decide. Each prompt routes to one skill in this plugin.

| Prompt | What you get | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| [Find Products With No Proof](find-products-with-no-proof.md) | A ranked list of products with no, thin, stale or text-only reviews, weighted by exposure. | [`review-coverage`](../../skills/review-coverage/SKILL.md) |
| [Rank Stale and Thin Reviews](rank-stale-and-thin-reviews.md) | High-exposure products whose reviews are too few or too old to reassure shoppers today. | [`review-coverage`](../../skills/review-coverage/SKILL.md) |
| [Place Reviews Where Shoppers Decide](place-reviews-where-shoppers-decide.md) | Where each piece of proof should appear, what it replaces, and how I'll know it worked. | [`proof-placement`](../../skills/proof-placement/SKILL.md) |
| [Reuse Customer Photos in Marketing](reuse-customer-photos-in-marketing.md) | A reuse plan for customer photos and video, limited to media with confirmed rights. | [`proof-placement`](../../skills/proof-placement/SKILL.md) |
| [Check Review Syndication Readiness](check-review-syndication-readiness.md) | Whether my reviews are ready to distribute beyond my store, decided separately from onsite display. | [`proof-placement`](../../skills/proof-placement/SKILL.md) |
