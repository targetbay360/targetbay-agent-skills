Using TargetBay Reviews (`proof-placement` skill), find what my customers consistently praise and decide where to amplify it.

First derive from my store data: review text and ratings by product and period, especially high ratings, and where proof currently appears.

Give me:
- recurring attributes praised, with frequency, products and verbatim quotes
- strengths my listings under-state or don't mention
- praise concentrated in one product line or period versus broad
- where each strength should appear, and what it displaces
- how I'll know each placement worked

Change nothing until I approve.
