Using TargetBay Reviews (`review-coverage` skill), find which of my products are short of reviews and which gaps matter most.

First derive from my store data: per-product review count, rating, recency and media, plus orders or traffic per product.

Give me:
- each gap: products, gap type (none, thin, stale, text-only, contested) and exposure
- the pattern behind it: new products, a category, a price band
- what closing each gap is worth, and on what evidence
- which skill owns each fix

If data is missing, say so and ask; don't estimate. Analysis only: change nothing and send nothing.
