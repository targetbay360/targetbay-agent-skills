# Content Rules

Subject lines, copy, offers and claims. Cited as `content-rules.md#N1`.

This package produces **content direction**, not finished creative, unless a skill explicitly says
otherwise. Direction means the message, the angle, the proof and the call to action — enough for a human
or a template to produce the final asset.

---

### N1. The message must match the audience's actual state.
A win-back message to someone who bought last week is a broken message. Content follows from the audience
definition, never the reverse.

### N2. One primary call to action.
A message with four equally weighted actions gets none of them. Secondary links are fine; second primary
CTAs are not.

### N3. Never make a claim the store cannot support.
No invented statistics, invented reviews, invented scarcity, invented deadlines. Urgency must be real —
a real end date, real stock, a real cut-off. See [global-rules.md#G3](global-rules.md).

### N4. Subject line and body must agree.
A subject line that promises something the body does not deliver buys one open and loses future ones.

### N5. Lead with the reason the message exists.
The recipient decides in about a second. Put the relevance — what it is, why them, why now — first.

### N6. Write for the channel.
Email can carry context and browsing. SMS carries one idea and one link. Do not port email copy into SMS.
See [../knowledge/sms-principles.md](../knowledge/sms-principles.md) and
[../knowledge/email-principles.md](../knowledge/email-principles.md).

### N7. Offers are stated exactly.
Amount, what it applies to, exclusions, expiry, and how it is redeemed. An ambiguous offer becomes a
support ticket and a chargeback.

### N8. Accessibility is a requirement, not a polish step.
Meaningful alt text, a readable text-to-image ratio, sufficient contrast, descriptive link text, and a
message that still works with images disabled.

### N9. Every message has a visible, working opt-out.
Including SMS, including transactional-adjacent marketing. Never obscure it.

### N10. Respect the brand's own voice.
Where a store has established voice and terminology, follow it. Where a playbook defines vertical
conventions, follow those. Do not import generic marketing register.

### N11. Do not recycle content across competing sends.
If two messages in the same window use the same angle to the same people, one of them is redundant.

### N12. Content decisions are testable.
When a content choice is a judgement call, mark it as a testable variable rather than presenting it as
settled. See [../knowledge/experimentation-principles.md](../knowledge/experimentation-principles.md).
